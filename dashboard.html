About The Scheme Setu Portal

Bridging Beneficiaries with Statutory Government Credit Schemes

Every year, substantial budgetary allocations under Central & State welfare initiatives remain underutilized due to paperwork friction, lack of transparent interest calculations, and reliance on unauthorized intermediaries.

Scheme Setu acts as an authoritative, zero-cost decision support layer. It determines your precise eligibility under verified government programs (NSFDC, PMEGP, MUDRA, CSIS, Padho Pardesh), models exact quarterly/monthly instalments, and connects you directly to the closest active partner bank branch.

100% Algorithmic Matching

Deterministic eligibility rules ensure zero human bias, zero fee extortion, and full compliance.

Real Cost Comparison

Shows exact interest savings compared to standard private or commercial bank lending rates.

Geo-Spatial Branch Routing

Directs application dossiers straight to accepting nodal public sector and rural bank branches.
